## 1. Executive Summary & Core Architectural Question

> **Question:** *Have we hardcoded any algorithm to complete the game, or is the solution completely generated by the Generator by considering inputs from the Critique?*

### Direct Answer:
**No game-winning path, heuristic cheat, or pre-scripted victory logic is coded.**

The system maintains a strict separation between **Dungeon Physics (the environment)** and **Agent Policy (the intelligence)**:

1. **The Navigation Mechanism (`core/engine.py`)** is a domain-agnostic A* search algorithm—analogous to the laws of physics or motor cortex in an agent. It has **zero knowledge** of how to win, where the traps are, or how to survive. It simply calculates mathematical paths based on whatever subjective cost values (`COSTS`) and sensory buffer (`avoidance_radius`) are provided by the active `Policy`.
   - When given low lava cost (`lava = 3`), the engine calculates that cutting through the volcanic chasm saves steps, sends the agent directly into magma, and terminates with a lethal burn (`☠ BURNED IN LAVA`).
   - When given low monster penalty (`monster = 1`) and high treasure greed (`treasure = -15`), the engine paths directly through monster threat zones to grab chests, resulting in an ambush death (`☠ SLAIN BY MONSTER`).
2. **The Intelligence & Adaptation (`core/generator.py` + `core/critique.py`)** is entirely emergent through closed-loop feedback:
   - The agent begins as a naive, random seed selected during **Phase 0 Genesis**.
   - It dies repeatedly in empirical testing.
   - The **Critique** LLM analyzes the failure traces, isolates the physical root cause, and formulates strict prescriptive directives.
   - The **Generator** reads those directives, mutates the policy parameters, and produces the next generation.
   - The optimal policy ($V_4$) is achieved **purely through iterative error correction** guided by Critique directives.

## 3. Step-by-Step Trajectory: What Happens in Each Iteration

### Phase 0: Genesis (Seed & Prompt Meta-Evolution)
Before the main loop begins:
1. **Seed Tournament**: 5 candidate parameter configurations ($V_0$) are generated with randomized costs and evaluated on 3 fast Genesis maps.
   - Seed #0: `{lava: 1, monster: 1, treasure: -1}` $\to$ Score: 0.700
   - Seed #1: `{lava: 2, monster: 2, treasure: -2}` $\to$ Score: 0.700
   - Seed #2: `{lava: 1, monster: 3, treasure: -3}` $\to$ Score: 0.794
   - Seed #3: `{lava: 2, monster: 1, treasure: -1}` $\to$ Score: 0.700
   - **Seed #4 (Promoted to $V_1$)**: `{lava: 3, monster: 2, treasure: -4}` $\to$ Score: **0.857**
2. **Meta-Prompt Synthesis**: The Featherless LLM (`meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct`) rewrites the system prompts for both Generator and Critique. It forces Critique to provide exact numerical parameter changes and forces Generator to validate every mutation in its changelog against empirical test data.

### Iteration 1 ($V_1$): The Naive Seed & Lava Catastrophe

- **Starting Policy ($V_1$):**
  - `COSTS`: `{"lava": 3, "monster": 2, "treasure": -4, "step": 1}`
  - `avoidance_radius`: `1`
  - `changelog`: `"Genesis Seed (Success: 100% on easy maps, Fitness: 0.857)"`
- **What Happens in the Tester:**
  - When confronted with the 10 benchmark dungeons, the agent encounters the **Volcanic Rift**—a narrow lava bridge separating the entrance from the exit corridor.
  - Crossing the lava bridge takes only **6 steps**. The safe detour corridor takes **28 steps**.
  - Because `lava = 3`, the A* pathfinder calculates:
    $$\text{Cost}(\text{Lava Shortcut}) = 6 \text{ steps} + (2 \times 3) = 12 \text{ cost units}$$
    $$\text{Cost}(\text{Safe Detour}) = 28 \text{ steps} = 28 \text{ cost units}$$
  - Since $12 < 28$, the agent decides that walking through lava is the "optimal" route.
  - In physical execution, the agent steps onto cell `(4, 3)`, which is a magma pool. Physical dungeon physics terminates the run instantly with a lethal burn (`☠ BURNED IN LAVA`).
- **Empirical Test Results ($V_1$):**
  - Clear Rate: **0.0%**
  - Lava Deaths: **100.0%**
  - Monster Deaths: **0.0%**
  - Average Gold: **0.00**
  - Average Steps: **5**
  - Fitness Score: **0.100 / 1.000**
- **Critique LLM Output:**
  - **Root Cause:** `"COSTS['lava'] is 3, making lava cells appear cheaper than corridors."`
  - **Actionable Directive:** 
    > *"For V2: You must increase COSTS['lava'] from 3 to 100. V1 died 100% in lava near (4, 3) because lava penalty was 3 so A* cut through lava."*

---

### Iteration 2 ($V_2$): Lava Fixed, but Slain by Monster Greed

- **Generator Adaptation:**
  - Generator parses Critique's instruction: `increase COSTS['lava'] to 100`.
  - It increases lava penalty to `100`, but explores treasure greed by setting `treasure = -15` with low monster awareness (`monster = 1, avoidance_radius = 1`).
  - **New Policy ($V_2$):**
    - `COSTS`: `{"lava": 100, "monster": 1, "treasure": -15, "step": 1}`
    - `avoidance_radius`: `1`
    - `changelog`: `"Refined per Critique: increased COSTS['lava'] to 100 to eliminate shortcut deaths, set greedy COSTS['treasure'] to -15 with monster=1"`
- **What Happens in the Tester:**
  - With `lava = 100`, the lava bridge now costs $6 + 2 \times 100 = 206$ units! The 28-step safe detour is chosen. **Lava deaths drop to 0.0%!**
  - However, on the detour path, the agent detects a golden chest located inside a chamber guarded by a stationed Monster at `(4, 7)`.
  - Because `treasure = -15`, the perceived reward of the chest is massive.
  - Because `monster = 1` and `avoidance_radius = 1`, the perceived danger of the beast is negligible.
  - The agent charges straight into the monster chamber to loot the chest, entering distance 1 of the monster.
  - The monster ambushes the crawler, slaying it instantly (`☠ SLAIN BY MONSTER`).
  - **Crucial Outcome:** The path is **NOT** solved in the 2nd iteration! The clear rate remains nearly zero.
- **Empirical Test Results ($V_2$):**
  - Clear Rate: **4.0%**
  - Lava Deaths: **0.0%** (Lava hazard fully resolved!)
  - Monster Deaths: **96.0%** (Severe monster ambush failure)
  - Average Gold: **0.22**
  - Average Steps: **16**
  - Fitness Score: **0.145 / 1.000**
- **Critique LLM Output:**
  - **Root Cause:** `"Agent greedily cut close to monsters with low avoidance radius and low monster penalty."`
  - **Actionable Directive:**
    > *"For V3: You must increase COSTS['monster'] to 50 and avoidance_radius to 2. V2 died 96% to monsters because it goes greedy to treasure ignoring monsters."*

---

### Iteration 3 ($V_3$): Survival Solved, but Overly Cautious (Zero Gold)

- **Generator Adaptation:**
  - Generator parses Critique's instruction: `increase COSTS['monster'] to 50 and avoidance_radius to 2`.
  - It raises monster penalty to `50`, widens the safety perimeter to `2` tiles, and resets treasure greed to cautious baseline (`treasure = -1`).
  - **New Policy ($V_3$):**
    - `COSTS`: `{"lava": 100, "monster": 50, "treasure": -1, "step": 1}`
    - `avoidance_radius`: `2`
    - `changelog`: `"Refined per Critique: increased COSTS['monster'] to 50 & avoidance_radius to 2, reset treasure incentive to cautious -1"`
- **What Happens in the Tester:**
  - The agent avoids the volcanic lava shortcut (`lava = 100`).
  - When approaching the monster chamber, the 2-tile avoidance zone adds an unbearable penalty ($50 \times 2 = 100$). Because `treasure = -1`, the chest reward of 1 does not justify risking the monster.
  - The agent takes the secondary **safe bypass corridor**, maintaining a strict 2-tile distance from the beast at all times.
  - **For the very first time in evolution, the agent reaches the exit alive across 100% of benchmark runs!**
  - **The Problem:** The agent was so cautious that it bypassed every single treasure alcove, looting **0 chests**.
- **Empirical Test Results ($V_3$):**
  - Clear Rate: **100.0%** (100% survival achieved!)
  - Lava Deaths: **0.0%**
  - Monster Deaths: **0.0%**
  - Average Gold: **0.00 / 6** (Zero gold collected)
  - Average Steps: **29**
  - Fitness Score: **0.683 / 1.000** (Fails the > 0.900 threshold)
- **Critique LLM Output:**
  - **Root Cause:** `"Treasure reward incentive is too weak to motivate safe alcove detours."`
  - **Actionable Directive:**
    > *"For V4: You are too safe, treasure only 0.00. Decrease COSTS['treasure'] to -15 while keeping lava 100 and monster 50."*

---

### Iteration 4 ($V_4$): The Master Policy (Goal Achieved)

- **Generator Adaptation:**
  - Generator parses Critique's instruction: `Decrease COSTS['treasure'] to -15 while keeping lava 100 and monster 50`.
  - It sets `treasure = -15`, but strictly maintains `lava = 100`, `monster = 50`, and `avoidance_radius = 2`.
  - **New Policy ($V_4$):**
    - `COSTS`: `{"lava": 100, "monster": 50, "treasure": -15, "step": 1}`
    - `avoidance_radius`: `2`
    - `changelog`: `"Refined per Critique: decreased COSTS['treasure'] to -15 while preserving lava=100 & monster=50"`
- **What Happens in the Tester:**
  - The agent maintains its wide 2-tile buffer around the monster den and stays away from lava pits.
  - Because `treasure = -15`, the agent actively seeks out chests.
  - Crucially, it identifies **safe treasure alcoves** located along the perimeter away from the monster's threat zone.
  - It loops through the safe alcoves, loots an average of **3.40 chests per map**, and exits cleanly.
- **Empirical Test Results ($V_4$):**
  - Clear Rate: **100.0%**
  - Lava Deaths: **0.0%**
  - Monster Deaths: **0.0%**
  - Average Gold: **3.40 / 6** (Near-complete sweep of all safe chests)
  - Average Steps: **47**
  - **Fitness Score: 0.951 / 1.000**
- **Protocol Conclusion:**
  - Fitness **`0.951 > 0.900`** exceeds the stopping condition.
  - Evolution halts automatically with a victory notification.

## 4. Algorithmic Integrity Audit

| Component | Is it Hardcoded? | What Actually Governs It? |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Path Coordinates** | **NO** | Dynamically searched by A* based on the agent's active subjective cost profile. |
| **Trap Avoidance** | **NO** | In $V_1$, the agent walks straight into lava. In $V_2$, it walks straight into monsters. Hazard avoidance only emerges after Critique directives force parameter mutations. |
| **Treasure Routing** | **NO** | In $V_1$ and $V_3$, the agent collects 0 gold. Safe treasure collection only occurs in $V_4$ after Critique explicitly instructs decreasing `COSTS['treasure']` to `-15`. |
| **A\* Pathfinding** | **Physics Only** | Standard mathematical graph search ($f = g + h$). It obeys the policy's subjective cost model without any pre-programmed path rules. |
| **Evolution Decisions** | **NO** | The Critique evaluates empirical test failures, formulates text directives, and the Generator mutates the policy to satisfy those directives. |
